Privacy

How Windward handles measurements, location and messages.

Effective 4 November 2026 · Contact: alexey.shtol@gmail.com

Who is responsible.

Windward is provided by Alexey Shtol. This policy covers the Apple Watch app, its complications, these support pages and messages you choose to send to the developer. Windward does not require a Windward account and includes no advertisements, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

What stays on your Watch.

The app stores pressure readings, altitude context, measurement times, preferences, last known coordinates, place names, forecast caches and notification state locally. The app and its complications share data through an app-group container. The app does not upload your raw pressure history to a Windward server, and it contains no separate advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking SDK.

Recorded pressure history is limited to approximately the latest 24 hours. Settings, last known location and caches can remain until replaced or app data is removed. watchOS and Apple control any system backups.

Location and external services.

With location permission, Windward obtains location for forecasts, solar events, nearby tide stations and GPS altitude when selected. Apple Core Location may also process location to resolve a place name. Forecast requests can reuse previously stored coordinates.

Services receive the network information needed for a connection, such as an IP address. Open-Meteo states that troubleshooting logs may include geographical coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. Its policy also describes technical IP logging. These request logs mean that location processing is not exclusively on your Watch.

Apple, NOAA and TidesAtlas handle service data under their own terms and privacy practices. TidesAtlas is used only when you select it and supply a key; that key can identify your provider account and is stored in the app’s persistent settings. Provider-side retention may differ from local app retention. Windward does not set those periods or promise immediate deletion by a provider.

Permissions and choices.

Motion access supports pressure measurement. Location access supports the functions described above. Notification permission enables optional local alerts. You can change these permissions in the system settings for the app.

Revoking location permission prevents new authorized fixes; it does not itself erase stored coordinates. Cached coordinates may still be used for forecast requests. Turning off automatic location updates does not guarantee that all network requests stop. Remove a provider key when you no longer want the optional keyed integration to use it.

Removing the app removes local app data through the normal watchOS process, subject to system backups. Contact the developer about local-data questions or a provider about its independently retained records. We do not claim control over another provider’s logs.

Support messages.

If you email the developer, your address, message and any attachments are processed to respond to your request and manage necessary follow-up. Do not send passwords, API keys or unrelated sensitive information. Gmail handles delivery and storage under Google’s service practices.

Correspondence is kept only for as long as needed to handle the request, necessary follow-up or applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by contacting the developer; any legally required retention and the mail provider’s backup practices may affect removal.

These web pages.

The pages have no advertising, analytics scripts, forms, embedded third-party content or externally loaded fonts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes; hosting-related handling is described in the GitHub privacy statement. Visiting an external policy link or choosing to email opens the relevant service.

Questions and rights.

Where applicable data-protection law gives you rights, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing by contacting alexey.shtol@gmail.com. You may also contact your local data-protection authority. Requests concerning a provider’s independent processing may need to be directed to that provider.

Permission-controlled features use your system authorization. Support correspondence is used to answer the request you initiate and handle necessary follow-up. The service providers’ own terms describe the basis and handling of their independent processing.

If the app’s processing changes, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated. The effective date above identifies this version.
