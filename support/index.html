Support

A little help for the instrument on your wrist.

Help for Windward · Last updated 4 November 2026

A direct line.

Windward is made by Alexey Shtol. For a question, a bug report or feedback, write to:

windward@shtol.com

Include your Watch model, watchOS version, Windward version and the steps that led to the issue. You can find the app version in Settings → About. Please leave out passwords, API keys and personal location details.

Start with pressure.

Windward is a standalone barometer for Apple Watch, with weather and astronomical information alongside your measurements. It requires watchOS 10.6 or later and a physical Watch with a barometric sensor for pressure readings. No companion iPhone app or Windward account is needed.

The app is free, with no advertising, subscriptions or in-app purchases. External data services have their own availability and terms; optional TidesAtlas integration uses your own API key.

Common questions.

Why is the pressure screen empty?

Allow motion access, open the Pressure screen and give the sensor time to deliver a reading. A simulator does not supply real barometer measurements. A new installation builds its history as readings arrive; it does not start with 24 hours of data.

How do I choose altitude correction?

In Settings → Pressure, choose raw pressure or correction to sea level. Height can come from the absolute altitude sensor, a GPS fix or a fixed value. Hardware availability, signal and permissions affect the available height. Raw pressure is recorded independently of height acquisition.

How can I support background collection?

Add a Windward complication to the watch face you use. This keeps pressure visible and can support more regular background updates. watchOS controls refresh timing: the selected interval is a preference, and continuous recording or exact alert delivery is not guaranteed.

Why are weather or tides unavailable?

Check connectivity, location permission and the selected providers. Weather coverage varies by region. NOAA tides are available for supported stations, mainly in the United States. TidesAtlas is optional and requires your own valid key. The barometer can keep working without an internet connection.

How do I check notifications?

Check system notification permission and the event switches in Windward. Settings → Test alert sends a clearly labelled local test. Test appearance does not guarantee the timing of future weather or background events.

Which languages and complications are available?

Windward supports English, Russian, German, French, Czech and Spanish and follows system language selection. Add pressure or rain complications to a compatible watch face or Smart Stack. Available layouts and refresh times depend on watchOS.

Your data and your choices.

Pressure history is stored on the Watch. Online forecast requests can send location to external providers. Read the privacy policy for the data flows, retention and controls.

Windward provides information to help you understand conditions. Forecasts, tides and alerts do not replace official warnings or navigation equipment.
